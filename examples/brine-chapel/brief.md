## Whole-site facts

- Dressed stone passages "about three paces wide": corridors 2.8 m.
- No map in the source. Relative positions come from the compass phrases in the room key; where the text is silent
  (the Tally Room, the Lower Winch) rooms are placed to keep passages short and straight.
- The text does not say which wall of the sump the passage to area 7 leaves by, only "south".
