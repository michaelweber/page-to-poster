# Mapping guidelines

What was asked for: "Map the chapel level of The Brine Chapel as a poster for the table. Stone and salt, not
industrial. One version I can show the players."

- **What to map:** the chapel level, areas 1-8 of `source/adventure.md`. The drowned workings below are not mapped.
- **Scale:** the text counts in paces; one pace is taken as one meter. Rooms without a size are estimated from
  what has to fit in them.
- **Orientation:** the text gives compass directions ("east of the nave", "north wall"); north is +y.
- **Look:** dressed stone for the cut rooms, raw rock for the grotto and the collapse, white salt for the altar,
  pillars, statues and crystals.
- **Poster size:** 36 x 24 inches, landscape, 200 dpi.
- **Colors:** brine-green paper, near-black ink, off-white, vermilion accent.
- **Versions:** gm, player, bare.
- **Must show:** the three ways in; the pump and the drain; the barred door; the blocked passage.
- **Must hide from players:** the pivoting slab and the passage behind it, the Ossuary's name, what is in the
  pool, Odile's bed and chest, the ledger's contents, the smugglers' cache.
- **Title and subtitle:** "The Brine Chapel" / "Harrow Deep salt workings, chapel level".
- **ComfyUI:** not needed. (`paint.json` is there for the optional room paintings.)
